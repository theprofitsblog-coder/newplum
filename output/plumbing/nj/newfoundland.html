When should I call a plumber instead of trying to fix it myself?
If the problem involves your main water line, sewer line, gas lines, or anything behind a wall, call a professional. Also call if a simple fix does not work after one or two attempts. Trying to force a repair can make the problem worse and more expensive.
My old home has outdated plumbing. Should I repipe?
Many older homes in the northeast have galvanized steel or even lead pipes that corrode over time. If you are seeing discolored water, low pressure, or frequent leaks, a repipe might be worth considering. A licensed plumber can inspect your system and advise you.
