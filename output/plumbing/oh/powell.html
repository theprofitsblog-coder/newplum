Can a plumber help with low water pressure?
Yes. Low water pressure can be caused by clogged aerators, corroded pipes, a failing pressure regulator, or issues with the municipal supply. A plumber can diagnose the cause and fix it.
How do I deal with hard water in my home?
Hard water is common across the midwest and causes mineral buildup in pipes, water heaters, and fixtures. A water softener can help protect your plumbing and appliances. A plumber can test your water hardness and recommend the right system.
