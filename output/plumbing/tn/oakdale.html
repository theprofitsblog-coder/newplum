How do I know if I have a hidden water leak?
Watch for signs like unexplained increases in your water bill, damp spots on walls or floors, mold or mildew smells, and the sound of running water when nothing is turned on. A plumber can do a pressure test to confirm a hidden leak.
What causes slow drains in this area?
