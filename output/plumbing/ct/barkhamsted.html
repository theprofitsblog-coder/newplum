Do you handle emergency plumbing calls?
Yes. Plumbing emergencies like burst pipes, major leaks, and sewer backups need fast attention. Call us and we will connect you with a plumber who can respond quickly.
Can a plumber help with low water pressure?
Yes. Low water pressure can be caused by clogged aerators, corroded pipes, a failing pressure regulator, or issues with the municipal supply. A plumber can diagnose the cause and fix it.
How do I protect my pipes from freezing in the winter?
Insulate exposed pipes in basements, crawl spaces, and exterior walls. Let faucets drip during extreme cold snaps. Keep your thermostat set to at least 55 degrees even when you are away. If a pipe does freeze, call a plumber right away before it bursts.
