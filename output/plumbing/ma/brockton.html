How do I protect my pipes from freezing in the winter?
Insulate exposed pipes in basements, crawl spaces, and exterior walls. Let faucets drip during extreme cold snaps. Keep your thermostat set to at least 55 degrees even when you are away. If a pipe does freeze, call a plumber right away before it bursts.
